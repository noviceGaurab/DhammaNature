What you unlock as a committed volunteer

Volunteers who show up reliably get priority access to future retreats, invitations to community gatherings, a verified "Serve with Dhamma" badge on your profile, written volunteering references, and 15 reward points added after your documents are verified - not before.

No-show consequence

Every seat at our centers is given to a genuine student. If you sign up and do not appear without giving prior notice to our email contact at least 24 hours ahead, you will be flagged as a no-show: your volunteer "Serve with Dhamma" badge is removed, you lose access to priority volunteering and retreat perks, and repeated no-shows may suspend your volunteer account.

How to cancel without a penalty

Email contact@dhammnature.example or use your profile at least 24 hours in advance with the opportunity name. Notices given before the deadline keep your record clean and your future opportunities open.

Step 2 - Prove you're a genuine student

A clear photo of your student ID card so our coordinator can confirm you are a student (JPG, PNG, WEBP - max 10MB).

A document on official letterhead showing your college logo / stamp approving you as a student volunteer. The visible logo and official stamp prove it is genuine and cannot be forged like a plain typed note.
