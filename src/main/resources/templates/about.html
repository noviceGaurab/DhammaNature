CONNECT, GROW AND SERVE WITH US

A way of life, not just a website

We are a community of monks, teachers and lay practitioners who share one conviction - that the Dhamma can be lived in every moment, not only on the cushion but at the market, in the family and amid the noise of ordinary days.

Through sermons, teachings, retreats, charity and a vibrant discussion community, we support one another toward wisdom, compassion and freedom from suffering.

DHAMMA'S LOVE

Beliefs and history

The Buddha's love is not a doctrine to memorise but a warmth to extend - to ourselves first, then to every being we meet, without exception.

A Monk should practice dhamma, teach laypeople when asked. Guide them to the truth.

Our mission is to keep the Dhamma freely available to all who seek it, and to guide practitioners step by step toward the peace that is already within them.
