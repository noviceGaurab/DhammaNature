FIVE AGGREGATES SERIES

Synopsis

The journey of self-discovery is a path filled with introspection and mindfulness. Each step brings new insights into the nature of existence. The teachings of the Five Aggregates help us understand the impermanence of form, sensation, perception, mental formations and consciousness - and to see, in that seeing, what we truly are.

How to make a teaching your own

Reading is only the beginning. Here is how to carry the dhamma from the page into practice.

Listen once

Read or listen without trying to memorise. Let the words wash over you like a gentle stream.

Reflect

Return to the passages that stayed with you. What does the teaching ask of you in this moment?

Practise

Choose one practice from the teaching and carry it into your next meditation session or daily task.

Discuss

Share what arose in practice with your community. Insight deepens when it is spoken aloud.
