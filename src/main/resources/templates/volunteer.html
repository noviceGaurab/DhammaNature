How it works

Getting involved takes just three steps.

1. Browse opportunities

Explore upcoming volunteering dates below, each hosted at one of our partner meditation centers.

2. Sign up in one click

Registered members can join directly. Signing up earns 15 reward points automatically.

3. Show up & serve

Arrive at the listed time, follow the coordinator's lead, and enjoy the joy that service brings.

Ways to volunteer

Food distribution, cleaning, tree plantation and religious events - and more.

Food Distribution

Package and serve warm meals to visitors, pilgrims and those in need.

Grounds & Temple Cleaning

Prepare temple grounds and halls ahead of festivals and retreats.

Tree Plantation Drives

Join reforestation and garden care around the retreat centers.

Religious Events

Assist with puja, retreats and community gatherings across the festival season.
