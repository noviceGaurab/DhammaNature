Dhamma will uplift your mentality

Thanissaro Bhikku · May 5, 2025

May all beings be at ease. Today we sit with a single question: how can the Dhamma uplift the mind that has become heavy?

The Buddha often reminded us that the mind shapes the world around it. When we train the mind to see clearly - to watch a thought arise, to meet it with understanding instead of fear - we begin to soften the grip of suffering.

Begin by sitting quietly for a few minutes. Notice your breath. When the mind wanders, name it gently: "thinking, thinking." Do not fight it. Simply return to the breath, again and again. This is not failure; this is practice.

Then widen your attention to the body, to the sounds, to the space around you. Notice how much of what troubles us is created by habit rather than by circumstance. The Dhamma teaches that with steady effort, even a river of worry can be crossed.

Carry this stillness into your day. Let the practice uplift your mentality - not by denying difficulty, but by meeting it with wisdom, patience and loving-kindness.

May you be well, may you be at peace.
