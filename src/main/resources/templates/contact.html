Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly will you reply to my message?

We aim to respond to every message within 24 hours during the working week.

Can I request a free dhamma book or recording?

Yes - most of our teachings are available free of charge. Browse the Teachings page to read or download.

How can I organise a group retreat?

Contact us with proposed dates and group size and our volunteers will arrange everything with a partner centre.

Prefer to talk in person?

Join us at any of our partner meditation centres - everyone is welcome, and no experience is needed to start.
