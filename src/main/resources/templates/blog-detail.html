Building a Healthy Lifestyle, One Mindful Step at a Time

August 28, 2025 Ajahn Brahm 2 comments 0 likes
Small, steady habits shape a life more than grand resolutions ever do. Begin with one mindful breath before each meal, one deliberate pause before answering, and one sincere good-night. As the Buddha taught, the path is not a leap but a walk - step by step, day by day.

Ajahn Brahm
