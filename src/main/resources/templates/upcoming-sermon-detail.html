Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join a live sermon?

For online sermons, a streaming link will be emailed to registered attendees 1 hour before the session. For in-person events, arrive at the listed venue at least 15 minutes early.

Can I ask questions during the talk?

Yes. Most sessions include a 15-minute Q&A segment at the end. Online attendees can type questions in the chat; in-person attendees will have a chance to raise their hand.

Are recordings available after the session?

Past sermons are recorded and made available on the upcoming sermons page. Check back after the session date for the recording link.

Do I earn reward points for attending?

Yes! Registered members earn 5 reward points for each attended session. Points are added automatically after the session is marked complete.
