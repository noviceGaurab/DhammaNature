Community guidelines

Be respectful

Speak kindly, even when you disagree. Treat every member as a guest in your home.

Stay on topic

Keep threads about the Dhamma and related practice. Post in the right category.

No spam

No promotional content, solicitation, or links to dubious sources in discussions.

Support each other

Answer questions patiently - someone's question today may be your own tomorrow.

Come join the conversation

Read a sutta, ask a question, or simply greet your fellow practitioners. The doors are always open.
